Privacy Policy

Finora – Expense & Income Tracker

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Finora ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Finora – Expense & Income Tracker mobile application ("App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand how information is handled when you use Finora.

1. Information We Collect

Finora is designed primarily as a personal finance tracking application. Depending on the features you use, the App may process information that you enter into the App.

Financial Information

You may voluntarily enter financial information such as:

  • Expense amounts
  • Income amounts
  • Transaction descriptions
  • Transaction dates
  • Expense and income categories
  • Budget information
  • Financial goals

This information is used to provide the tracking, reporting and budgeting features of the App.

2. How Your Financial Data Is Used

Information that you enter into Finora may be used to provide and improve features such as:

  • Expense tracking
  • Income tracking
  • Transaction history
  • Budget management
  • Financial summaries
  • Spending reports and charts
  • Search and filtering
Important: Finora does not provide financial, investment, tax, accounting or legal advice. Information displayed by the App is intended for personal tracking and informational purposes only.

3. Data Storage

Depending on the version and features of the App, your information may be stored locally on your device or through services used to provide optional backup, synchronization or other App functionality.

You should maintain appropriate backups of information that is important to you.

4. Advertising

Finora may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services.

These advertising providers may collect certain information about your device and interaction with advertisements in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Advertising services may use technologies such as cookies, mobile advertising identifiers or similar technologies to provide, measure and improve advertising.

Where applicable, you can manage advertising and privacy choices through the settings provided by your device and the relevant advertising provider.

5. Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how users interact with Finora and to improve the App.

Analytics information may include technical information such as application version, device type, operating system version, general usage information and crash information.

Analytics information is intended to help us identify problems, improve performance and develop better features.

6. Third-Party Services

Finora may use third-party services to support certain features, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or other functionality.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Examples may include advertising, analytics, cloud, hosting and application infrastructure providers.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Finora.

However, no method of electronic storage, transmission or communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal financial information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to operate, maintain, secure or improve the App.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, regulation or governmental request.

9. Children's Privacy

Finora is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. Your Choices

Depending on the features available in the App, you may be able to:

  • Edit or delete transactions
  • Remove information stored in the App
  • Disable certain permissions through your device settings
  • Manage advertising preferences through your device settings

11. Account and Data Deletion

If Finora provides account-based services, you may request deletion of your account and associated information by contacting us.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention or operational purposes.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Finora, our services, applicable laws or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact us.
